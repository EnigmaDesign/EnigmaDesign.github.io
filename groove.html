The Problem

Finding the right venue takes over 30 minutes.

72% of surveyed users struggled to find the right place to go on a Saturday night. The existing tools each fail in a different way — and none of them were built for this.

🔍

Google doesn't help

Search results are cluttered with closed venues, outdated hours, and zero real-time event data. You can't discover what you don't already know to search for.

Instagram is hit or miss

Problem Validation

We didn't build on assumptions. We built on data.

Before any design work started, I ran a structured research phase to validate the problem at scale — 500+ respondents, qualitative interviews, and direct competitive benchmarking. Every product decision that followed is grounded in what that research revealed.

📊

500+ quantitative data points

I designed and distributed a survey to over 500 respondents to test the core hypothesis: is nightlife discovery actually broken? 72% confirmed the pain point — independently, without prompting. The problem is real and widespread.

72% confirmed pain · 500+ respondents
🗣️

Qualitative user interviews

Led focus groups and 1:1 interviews to go beneath the quantitative surface — surfacing latent needs, emotional friction, and the specific moments where existing tools broke down. These sessions generated the functional requirements that shaped the PRD.

Focus groups · 1:1 interviews · PRD

Competitive gap analysis

Mapped the existing landscape — Google, Instagram, Spotify, dedicated venue apps — and identified exactly where each one failed the user. The gap wasn't a missing feature. It was a missing category: AI-driven real-time discovery built specifically for nightlife.

Category gap identified
The Solution

Groove: AI-powered nightlife, instantly.

A map-based discovery app that acts as a digital PR for clubs. Groove's AI recommendation engine matches users to the right venue in seconds — not minutes.

🤖

AI Recommendation Engine

Combines Spotify listening data, past behavior, and real-time event signals to surface the exact venue a user will actually enjoy — not just the nearest one.

🗺️

Map-based Discovery

See nearby events, live DJ sets, and active venues on an interactive map — filterable by music genre, entry price, dresscode, or vibe.

📋

Detailed Club Profiles

DJ lineup, ticket price, dress code, average age, music genre — know exactly what to expect before you even leave the house.

🎟️

In-App Ticketing

Multiple release tiers, real-time availability, and instant purchase — no third-party redirects, no queues at the door.
